# IK Digital Solutions — website reference study

Reviewed 16 September 2026. Research and proposed design direction; not a published redesign.

Visited all four homepages in the browser and inspected Elemental's project grid and Mono's project presentation. Mono Studio is a provisional interpretation of the name “Mono”; the user has not confirmed its URL.

## 1. Viralweb Africa — positioning

Source: [Viralweb Africa](https://viralweb.africa/).

The site identifies itself with East Africa and systems engineering. Its services connect regional business needs with commerce, local payments, operational software and WhatsApp workflows. Geography and the customer's operating context give the proposition specificity.

Application to IK: state the audience and practical job clearly. Focus on businesses in the DRC and beyond, French/English delivery, customer inquiries, orders and everyday operations. Keep technology details on service and project pages. Describe only capabilities IK can demonstrate or scope accurately.

## 2. Nimble AppGenie — messaging style

Source: [Nimble AppGenie](https://www.nimbleappgenie.com/).

The writing is confident, directed at the customer, and organized around business benefits. Service explanations lead toward a consultation; project examples, reviews and claims of expertise provide reassurance. Headings are short and emphatic, while supporting paragraphs explain the offer.

Application to IK: use the sequence **customer goal → specific deliverable → relevant example → next step**. Write active sentences and concrete calls to action. Support credibility with IK's own live work and clearly labelled demonstrations. The copy below is original, adopting the approach rather than reusing their sentences or credentials.

## 3. Elemental — portfolio cards

Source: [Elemental](https://www.elemental.agency/).

The observed desktop grid has unequal columns and varied card heights. Large photographic backgrounds, dark/magenta overlays, small category pills, prominent project names and brief functional descriptions make each project quickly identifiable. Cards lead to project stories.

Application to IK: let each screenshot occupy most of the card. Use a project name, one service category, a visible client/demo label and a single sentence describing its purpose. Make the project story the main destination; keep the live-site and demo choices clearly labelled within the story. Use a simple stacked layout on mobile.

## 4. Mono Studio — minimalist design

Source: [Mono Studio](https://mono.studio/en/).

The observed homepage uses oversized regular-weight typography, charcoal and light neutral surfaces, generous spacing, a compact pill navigation and a small yellow contact accent. Large project visuals carry much of the presentation, with sparse titles and service labels. Minimalism here comes from hierarchy and restraint; the page also uses video and animation.

Application to IK: reduce decorative boxes, establish a consistent spacing scale, give headings room and reserve a single accent for actions. Keep navigation and project information readable without depending on animation. Adapt the hierarchy to IK's identity and bilingual content.

## Recommended direction for IK

Retain the approved routes and service architecture. Prioritize this visual and editorial pass:

1. Rewrite the hero around the customer's work and a specific deliverable.
2. Enlarge the three selected project presentations; move technical lists into their stories.
3. Give each of the four service offers one clear benefit and one next step.
4. Simplify section backgrounds, borders and repeated explanatory paragraphs.
5. Preserve language pairs, contact context, client/demo distinctions and accessible mobile navigation.

Suggested homepage order: **business promise → selected work → four services → delivery process → project inquiry**. Keep detailed budgets on service pages and founder detail on About, with concise homepage links.
