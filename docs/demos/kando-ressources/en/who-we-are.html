A Congolese mining company, rooted in Kolwezi since 2009.

Kando Ressources SA is a company under Congolese law, 90% owned by Kando Holdings and 10% by the Congolese State under the 2018 Mining Code. We employ 2,340 people, 93% of them Congolese.

Kolwezi, 1973 · Anefo, CC0

Our mission

To produce copper and cobalt whose origin and production conditions leave no question unanswered: for our customers, for the State, for our neighbours.

Our commitments

Safety

Nobody should be hurt working for us. Life-saving rules apply to everyone, employees and contractors.

Transparency

Production, payments to the State and incidents are published on a fixed calendar.

Local employment

Hiring and purchasing first in Lualaba, training so that everyone can progress.

Respect

Communities are consulted before every project; every grievance is answered within 30 days.

Our history

  • 2009

    Exploration permit granted over the Kando deposit, 28 km from Kolwezi.

  • 2013

    Bankable feasibility study: 42 Mt of reserves at 2.4% Cu and 0.31% Co.

  • 2016

    First copper cathode produced at the Kando plant (SX-EW, 35,000 t/yr).

  • 2019

    Plant expanded to 65,000 t/yr with a cobalt hydroxide circuit.

  • 2022

    20 MWp solar plant commissioned, the first on a Lualaba mine site.

  • 2024

    Site certified ISO 45001; Copper Mark assurance process launched.

  • 2025

    Record output: 61,400 t of copper cathode. First independently assured sustainability report published.

Governance

The board has four members, including an independent director who chairs the audit committee. A health, safety, environment and communities committee meets quarterly.
