Frequent questions

  • What size of parcel can I send?

    Up to 30 kg and 70 × 50 × 40 cm per parcel, within a city or between cities. Beyond that, our freight service takes up to 500 kg per shipment, on quotation.

  • How do I write an address in Kinshasa?

    Commune, district, avenue and number where possible, then a landmark: church, school, bus stop, gate colour. The courier calls the recipient before arriving.

  • Can I schedule a pickup?

    Yes, up to seven days ahead, in two-hour slots. Business accounts can schedule recurring pickups.

  • What if the recipient is not there?

    The courier calls twice and waits 10 minutes. Free second attempt the next day, or collection at a relay point. After two failures the parcel is kept five days, then returned.

  • I did not receive a tracking number.

    It is sent by SMS as soon as the booking is confirmed and shown in the app. Check the phone number entered, or message us on WhatsApp with the sender's name.

  • Why is my intercity parcel not moving?

    Air parcels wait for the next available cargo flight. A postponed flight delays delivery by a day; you receive an SMS as soon as it leaves.

  • Can I change the delivery address?

    Yes, as long as the parcel is not out for delivery, from the tracking page or by WhatsApp. Within a city, a change of zone may add 3,000 FC.

  • Which payment methods do you accept?

    M-Pesa, Orange Money, Airtel Money, Afrimoney, Visa and Mastercard, or cash to the courier. Business accounts receive a monthly invoice.

  • How does cash on delivery work?

    The courier collects the amount from the recipient, in cash or mobile money. We pay it out within 24 hours, less a 2% fee (minimum 1,000 FC).

  • Are prices in francs or dollars?

    In Congolese francs, all taxes included. The dollar equivalent is shown for information, at 2,850 FC to 1 USD.

  • How do I open a business account?

    Fill in the form on the Business page. An adviser calls you within one working day; have your RCCM, national ID number and the manager's identity document ready.

  • Do you offer an API?

    Yes: shipment creation, rates, tracking, labels and webhooks. The test environment is free; documentation is sent on request to api@kimia-express.cd.

  • My parcel arrived damaged.

    Refuse it if you can, or take photos in front of the courier. File a claim within 48 hours with the tracking number.

  • How am I compensated?

    Up to the declared value, capped at 500,000 FC without extra insurance. Paid by mobile money within ten working days of agreement.

  • A courier asked me for extra money.

    The price paid at booking is final. Report it to customer service straight away: it is serious misconduct.

Prohibited and restricted items

Prohibited

  • Cash and foreign currency
  • Weapons, ammunition and explosives
  • Fuel, gas and flammable goods
  • Narcotics
  • Live animals
  • Minerals, stones and geological samples without CEEC documents

Accepted with conditions

  • Medicines: with pharmacy receipt
  • Lithium batteries: inside a device, never alone by air
  • Fresh food: within a city only
  • Items worth over 5,000,000 FC: on quotation, insurance required
