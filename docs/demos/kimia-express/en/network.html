How your parcels travel

Everything goes through the Masina hub: night sorting, trucks leaving at 5 am, loading onto the morning cargo flights.

Three ways to get there

Motorbike

In town: 620 couriers in Kinshasa, 90 in Lubumbashi, 70 in Goma. A motorbike gets through where cars wait.

Road

Daily truck on the N1 to Matadi and Boma, three times a week to Kikwit. Shuttles Lubumbashi – Kolwezi and Goma – Bukavu.

Air

Freight on domestic flights from N'djili: daily to Lubumbashi and Goma, three to five times a week to other cities. Barge for heavy parcels, on quotation.
