Volumetric weight

A light but bulky parcel takes the space of several parcels in the truck or plane. We charge the higher of actual and volumetric weight.

Options and fees

Insurance

Included up to 500,000 FC declared value. Above that: 1% of the additional value.

Cash on delivery

2% of the amount collected, minimum 1,000 FC. Paid out within 24 hours.

Relay point

Drop off or collect at a relay point: –1,000 FC within a city, –5,000 FC between cities.

Waiting

10 minutes of waiting included, then 1,000 FC per 10 minutes.

New attempt

Second attempt free the next day. Third attempt: 50% of the original price.

Return to sender

At the original shipment price. Kept free for five days at a relay point.

Payment methods

Paid by the sender at booking, or by the recipient on delivery.
