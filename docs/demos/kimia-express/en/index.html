News

What's new at Kimia

All news
  • 1 September 2026Cash on delivery is now paid out within 24 hoursProduct

    Amounts collected by our couriers are paid to shops the day after delivery, via M-Pesa, Orange Money, Airtel Money or bank transfer. The delay used to be seven days.

    Accounts shipping more than 1,000 parcels a month are paid every day at 6 pm.

  • 14 July 2026Mbandaka joins the network: air and bargeNetwork

    Three cargo flights a week now serve Mbandaka. Parcels over 100 kg leave Kinshasa by barge, on quotation, in eight to twelve days.

    The Boutique Bolingo relay point, near the port, receives and hands over parcels Monday to Saturday.

  • 3 June 2026Thirty electric motorbikes on trial in GombeEnvironment

    For six months, thirty couriers in Gombe and Lingwala ride electric motorbikes charged at the hub and the Gombe office, with swappable batteries.

    We are measuring real range, cost per delivery and breakdowns before deciding on a wider roll-out.
