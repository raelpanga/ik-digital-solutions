About

Kimia means “peace” in Lingala. We started in 2019 with six motorbikes and a WhatsApp number, so that nobody in Kinshasa would have to cross the city to drop off a document again.

Our mission

Make delivery as simple as a phone call: a clear price, a reliable courier, a parcel that arrives, anywhere in the DRC.

Our story

  • 2019

    Six motorbikes, a WhatsApp number and an office in Gombe. First document deliveries for law firms and banks.

  • 2020

    Groceries, meals and medicines delivered during the lockdown. 40 couriers.

  • 2021

    First e-commerce contracts with cash on delivery. Masina warehouse.

  • 2022

    Matadi and Boma open by road. Mobile app and SMS tracking.

  • 2023

    Air freight from N'djili. Lubumbashi, Goma and Bukavu offices.

  • 2024

    API for online shops. Kisangani, Mbuji-Mayi and Kananga. One million parcels in the year.

  • 2025

    Kolwezi, Kikwit and Mbandaka. 2.1 million parcels. Kimia Moto programme for couriers.

  • 2026

    Cash on delivery paid out within 24 hours. Thirty electric motorbikes on trial.

Why motorbikes

In Kinshasa a ten-kilometre trip can take two hours by car. On a motorbike, a trained courier does it in forty minutes, and can deliver in districts where the streets are not paved.

News

  • 1 September 2026Cash on delivery is now paid out within 24 hoursProduct

    Amounts collected by our couriers are paid to shops the day after delivery, via M-Pesa, Orange Money, Airtel Money or bank transfer. The delay used to be seven days.

    Accounts shipping more than 1,000 parcels a month are paid every day at 6 pm.

  • 14 July 2026Mbandaka joins the network: air and bargeNetwork

    Three cargo flights a week now serve Mbandaka. Parcels over 100 kg leave Kinshasa by barge, on quotation, in eight to twelve days.

    The Boutique Bolingo relay point, near the port, receives and hands over parcels Monday to Saturday.

  • 3 June 2026Thirty electric motorbikes on trial in GombeEnvironment

    For six months, thirty couriers in Gombe and Lingwala ride electric motorbikes charged at the hub and the Gombe office, with swappable batteries.

    We are measuring real range, cost per delivery and breakdowns before deciding on a wider roll-out.

  • 18 March 2026600 couriers certified in road safetySafety

    All Kinshasa couriers completed the two-day course: defensive riding, helmet and vest, carrying loads, riding in the rain.

    Lost-time accidents fell by 41% over twelve months.

  • 10 February 20262.1 million parcels delivered in 2025Results

    Volume doubled in a year, driven by online retail in Kinshasa and air freight to Lubumbashi and Goma. 96.8% of parcels were delivered within the announced time.

    Kimia has 3,400 business customers and 1,450 couriers, agents and staff.

  • 5 November 2025New app: live tracking and shareable linkProduct

    On delivery day the recipient sees the courier's position and receives a tracking link by SMS, without installing the app.

    Senders can reschedule a delivery or redirect it to a relay point in two clicks.
