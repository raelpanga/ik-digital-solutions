Become a courier

Paid for every delivery, paid out every Friday, insurance and equipment provided. We are recruiting in Kinshasa, Lubumbashi and Goma.

ApplyOther jobs

Why ride with Kimia

Clear income

2,200 FC on average per delivery in town, rates published in the app. Bonus of 25,000 FC a week above 95% on-time deliveries.

Paid every Friday

Paid out via M-Pesa, Orange Money or Airtel Money. No sign-up fee, ever.

Protected

Approved helmet, vest, rain gear and accident insurance provided. Two-day road safety course.

Free

You choose your hours and zones. No speed bonus: we pay for punctuality, not risk-taking.

Estimate your income

Example calculation for a courier in Kinshasa. Actual amounts depend on the deliveries you accept.

Motorbike

What you need

  • 18 or older
  • Category A driving licence
  • Voter card or passport
  • Android smartphone
  • Motorbike in good condition, or the Kimia Moto programme
  • Certificate of good conduct

Kimia Moto

Kimia Moto

No motorbike? We rent you one, new and serviced, for 45,000 FC a week. After 78 weeks, it is yours.

How it works

1. Apply

Form below or at the Masina hub. Reply by SMS within 48 hours.

2. Interview

Document check, interview and riding test at the hub.

3. Training

Two days: road safety, the app, customer service, cash on delivery.

4. First round

With a team leader, then you ride on your own.
