Services

Audit & advice

Have a website, application or project examined before you invest: real condition, risks, priorities and budget.

For your business

For owners inheriting a system, choosing between repair and rebuild, or seeking an independent view before signing a quote.

Discuss your project →

What you receive

  • Review of the site or application: speed, mobile use, accessibility, search visibility, basic security
  • Review of code, access, backups and dependencies
  • Compliance points to examine: Digital Code, personal data, required notices
  • A priority-ordered report with effort and budget estimates
  • A debrief with your team and an action plan at three, six and twelve months

How we work

A report that belongs to you

You receive a priority-ordered document: what is urgent, what can wait, what your own team can fix without us. It stays usable with any supplier, including the one who built the system.

Frequently asked questions

Do we then have to work with you?

No. The report is yours and remains usable with any supplier. We indicate what can be fixed in-house.

How long does an audit take?

From a few days for a brochure site to two weeks for a business application. Timing and scope are agreed before we start.
