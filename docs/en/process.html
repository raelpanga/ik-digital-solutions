Our process

You know what is in progress, what needs your review and what will be handed over.

A project in five steps

  1. Scope

    We define the problem, users, budget and acceptance criteria.

  2. Design

    You review the structure and screens before development.

  3. Build & test

    You try intermediate versions on a test link and share feedback.

  4. Launch

    We prepare access, checks and a rollback procedure.

  5. Support

    We agree on updates, backups and support after delivery.

Practical deliverables for working together

Before development

Written scope, page structure, mockups and acceptance criteria. Required features and external services are identified.

Before launch

A test version, mobile checks, contact journeys and access review. The release plan explains how to return to the previous version.

At handover

Source code, agreed accounts and access, documentation and onboarding. Responsibilities are explicit.

After handover

Fixes, improvements and support under the contract. Response times and recurring fees are defined in the chosen service.

Hosting, access and security

We define access, backup needs, handled data and hosting constraints before launch. Specific commitments depend on the system and are written into the contract.

This portfolio and its static demonstrations are published on GitHub Pages. Examples alone do not establish that a monitoring service or a particular hosting arrangement is operated for your business.
