Our process

  1. Scope

    We define the problem, users, budget and acceptance criteria.

  2. Design

    You review the structure and screens before development.

  3. Build & test

    You try intermediate versions on a test link and share feedback.

  4. Launch

    We prepare access, checks and a rollback procedure.

  5. Support

    We agree on updates, backups and support after delivery.
