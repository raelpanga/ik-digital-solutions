Method

How we deploy

Five steps, always in this order. You see a working version every two weeks.

  1. Scoping

    1 week

    A workshop with you. We write what the system must do, in plain language, with screens sketched together.

  2. Design

    1 to 2 weeks

    Clickable mockups tested on an Android phone under 100 USD and on a throttled 3G connection.

  3. Build

    2-week cycles

    You test each version on a staging link. What you approve goes to production, the rest is fixed.

  4. Deployment

    1 day

    Zero-downtime release. Data in the DRC, immediate backup, rollback procedure ready.

  5. Monitoring

    continuous

    Probes, alerts, fixes and a monthly report. That is the subscription, and it is what keeps you from calling us in an emergency.

Compliance & security

The Digital Code, written into our contracts.

Since 2023, Congolese law governs data hosting and processing. We treat it as a deliverable, not a footnote.

  • Data hosted in the DRCOrdinance-Law 23/010, the Digital Code. Your production data stays in Kinshasa; only encrypted backup copies are replicated outside the country.
  • ARPTC declaration prepared with youPersonal-data processing must be declared to or authorised by the regulator. We prepare the file with you.
  • HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, access logsNo exceptions, including internal dashboards.
  • Encrypted nightly backups, restore tested every quarterThe date of the last restore test appears in the monthly report delivered to the client.
  • You own itThe source code, the data, the domain name and the hosting accounts. We hand over every credential at delivery.
