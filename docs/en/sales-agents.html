The role

You work in the field, in your city: you spot the businesses that need to be online and show them what we build.

We take over scoping, the quote and the signature, and you stay informed at every step.

You work alone on site, with the team reachable on WhatsApp.

Your missions

  • Spot the shops, schools, practices, distributors and hotels that need a website or an application
  • Present our services and show our demos from your phone
  • Understand the need: what exists, what they want, who decides
  • Arrange the introduction to us on WhatsApp
  • Follow the deal to signature and keep the relationship with the client

How we work together

  1. You spot and present

    You choose the businesses to meet and show them our examples.

  2. We scope and quote with the client

    We run the scoping meeting and prepare the quote.

  3. Signature, then you keep the relationship

    The client signs with us; you remain their first contact on site.

Your profile

  • You know the merchants and businesses of your city
  • You are comfortable approaching a manager and explaining an offer simply
  • You are reachable on WhatsApp and organised enough to follow several deals
  • You handle a smartphone with ease
  • No technical skills are required: technical questions come back to us

What we provide

A presentation ready to show, online demonstrations, a contact reachable on WhatsApp, and a walkthrough of the offer before your first meetings.

How we hire

    Through the form below or on WhatsApp.
