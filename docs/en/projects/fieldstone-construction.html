Independent portfolio project for a fictional brand. Photography and video are illustrative stock media, not client construction work.

The study intention

Fieldstone Construction is a fictional brand created for this portfolio. The design challenge was to make home remodeling and rural construction equally clear from the moment a visitor arrives.

Visitors should recognize their kind of project quickly and have an obvious next step.

Our contribution

  • Visual identity and editorial direction for the concept brand
  • Video-led homepage with centered headline and calls to action
  • Separate pages for home remodeling and rural construction
  • Project inspiration gallery, mobile layout and scroll motion

Designing for everyday use

  • Both service paths visible on the homepage and in navigation
  • Readable text over video with an accessible playback control
  • Motion adjusted for reduced-motion preferences

What you can explore

Explore the video-led homepage, separate service journeys, project inspiration and a sample project planner. No real quote is sent.
