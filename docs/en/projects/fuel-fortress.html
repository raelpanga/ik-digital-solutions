Discuss your project
This project was delivered to a real client. The site is live at the address shown: open it and check for yourself what is described here.

Context and need

Present the identity and spaces of Fuel Fortress Lexington, then help prospective members discover the equipment, classes, recovery facilities and membership options.

Our contribution

  • A video-led homepage with expressive typography and a playback control
  • Photo galleries and a continuous equipment showcase with navigation controls
  • Sections for training, combat classes, the sauna, showers and apparel
  • Membership options linked to Gym Insight and an email-based tour request

Designing for everyday use

  • Navigation adapted to phones and larger screens
  • Direct routes to the spaces, classes, recovery facilities and memberships
  • Address, staffed hours and a directions link gathered in the visit section

What you can explore

Explore the spaces, equipment, combat classes, recovery facilities and Fuel Fortress apparel. Membership offers open the Gym Insight enrollment journey; the tour request prepares an email to send to the team.

Open production site

Delivery and operation

  • A public English-language website hosted on Vercel and served over HTTPS.
  • Enrollment opens Gym Insight; tour requests are prepared in the visitor’s email app.
