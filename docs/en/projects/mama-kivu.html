The study intention

Mama Kivu is an original design study exploring a digital presence for the restaurant sector. It does not represent a client business or commercial results.

The work balances identity, useful information and a simple first step for a visitor.

Our contribution

  • Filterable menu
  • Food photography
  • Booking journey
  • Real photography, responsive layout and French version

Designing for everyday use

  • A hierarchy that remains clear on a phone
  • Content and navigation that can adapt to French and English
  • Contact or booking journeys shaped for the business

What you can explore

The demonstrator presents journeys with fictional data. Payments, messages and integrations marked as simulated do not perform real operations.
