Privacy

Information about this portfolio · updated 16 September 2026.

Browsing the portfolio

The portfolio code does not implement visitor accounts, advertising trackers or audience analytics. Pages and resources are requested from the host; providers may maintain technical request logs.

Fonts and resources

The site's fonts and images are served alongside the pages, without calling a third-party service.

Contact form

Form fields are sent to Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form delivery service, which forwards them to us by e-mail at consultation@iksolutions-inc.com. The portfolio does not keep those fields on its pages or in browser storage. The WhatsApp button prepares a message in your app, which you confirm yourself.

After your message

When you send a message, the information received is used to respond and discuss your project. For questions about these exchanges or a deletion request, write to consultation@iksolutions-inc.com.

Demonstrators

Some demonstrations use browser local storage for scenarios, preferences or simulated operations. Use fictional data. You can clear this data in your browser settings; some examples also provide a reset action.

External services

Web3Forms, WhatsApp, your email provider, Vercel and external production sites apply their own data-handling practices. This page describes the portfolio, not those services.
