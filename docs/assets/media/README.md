# Portfolio photography

These images are locally optimized WebP copies of photographs already used in the
Kimia demonstration. They illustrate the place; they are not photographs of IK staff,
offices or clients. No competitor imagery is used.

- `kinshasa-night.webp`: **Kinshasa by night**, MONUSCO Photos,
  [original source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kinshasa_by_night_(23769991270).jpg),
  [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/).
- `gombe.webp`: **La Gombe, Kinshasa**, ByaduniaEspoir,
  [original source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:La_Gombe,_Kinshasa,_RDC.jpg),
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: resized/recompressed as WebP; responsive cropping and dark overlays are
applied in CSS. The photograph copies remain available under their respective
licenses. Public credit links appear beside the photographs and in Legal → Credits.
